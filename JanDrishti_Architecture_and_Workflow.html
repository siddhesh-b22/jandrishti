Weight: 35%
3. Vendor Cartel (HHI) HHI = Σ (Market Share)² (> 2500)
Weight: 25%
4. Velocity Spikes Z-Score > 3.0 (March-rush spike)
Weight: 20%
5. Predictive Delay RandomForestRegressor(District Track)

🛡️ Board 5: Statutory Authority Matrix (Separation of Powers)
Clear demarcation of powers and territorial boundaries across Indian public finance tiers.
Role & Rank Jurisdiction Scope Statutory Powers (What they CAN do) Strict Restrictions (What they CANNOT do)
RANK 1
MINISTRY_ADMIN		 Pan-India
(National Corpus)		 View all 36 States, configure system risk weights, approve central dockets, manage system users, review national audit alerts. Cannot modify ground physical milestone records; cannot bypass district techno-economic feasibility.
RANK 2
STATE_NODAL		 Designated State
(user.state)		 Review district dockets, grant statutory Financial Sanction (AS/FS), reject non-compliant works, manage state budget quotas. Cannot access, approve, or modify records of any other Indian State.
RANK 3
DISTRICT_AUTHORITY		 Designated District
(user.district)		 Techno-economic scrutiny, tender floating, contractor assignment, milestone updates, geo-tagged photo uploads, release final payment. Cannot recommend works; cannot sanction outside district; cannot modify treasury transaction values directly.
RANK 4
MEMBER OF PARLIAMENT		 Parliamentary Seat
(user.mp_id)		 Draft and submit work recommendations within ₹5 Crore entitlement; track execution velocity; view constituency ledger. Locked from editing after submission; cannot approve works; cannot access other MPs' dockets.
RANK 5
STATUTORY AUDITOR		 National Mandate
(CAG / Independent)		 Inspect Benford flags, cartel HHI scores, duplicate work dockets; create forensic audit cases; flag transactions for recovery. Read-only to financial ledgers; cannot disburse, approve, or alter financial figures.
RANK 6
GENERAL CITIZEN		 Open Public
(National Read-Only)		 Inspect all works on interactive maps; search MP utilization; scan QR code plaques on physical assets; submit ground discrepancy feedback. Zero mutation power on official records; cannot edit or sanction works.
🔒 Absolute Financial Immutability Policy (ABAC)

Under backend/rbac_abac.py, fields including sanctioned_amount, expenditure_amount, work_id, and internal_transaction_id strictly reject generic HTTP PUT/PATCH updates. They can only be updated via verified Treasury Voucher reconciliation ledger operations.
